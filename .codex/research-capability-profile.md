# Research Capability Profile

project: Software/wiki
research_level: medium
upstream_owner: AcknowledgeBase
status: active

## Purpose

Software/wiki is a template-source knowledge workspace. It should call upstream research-capability patterns and keep a repo-local execution entry, without copying the full AcknowledgeBase Research OS as a parallel source of truth.

## Required Capability

- call upstream research-capability: use AcknowledgeBase as the source standard and keep local adaptations inside `skills/research-capability`, `skills/technology-research`, governance rules, templates, and checker.
- Research Contract: object, decision goal, depth, evidence plan, output shape, and no-op boundary.
- Source Ledger: external links, local raw / source ledger, read status, and refresh trigger for durable research assets.
- currentness: verify release, license, platform, API, policy, security, and ecosystem facts before current claims.
- Judgment states: confirmed / observed / reported / inferred / blocked.
- So-What: state what a research result changes for template adoption, project design, or continued observation.
- deal-breaker: record license, supply-chain, privacy, cost, quality, or maintenance blockers.
- local research note: keep wiki-specific research outputs in local article, concept, project, or template owners.
- decision back-link: link adopted conclusions back to their owner page and upstream source.
- no-op: short-lived signals may remain out of durable docs if they do not change a decision.

## Upgrade Trigger

Escalate to strong only if this repo becomes the primary source for cross-project research rules, sensors, or Research OS standards.
