## Purpose

Software/wiki is the template-kernel owner for reusable research execution contracts. It absorbs the complete transferable Research OS capability into repo-local skills, templates, governance and evaluators, while AcknowledgeBase remains the upstream design and domain-knowledge owner. Strong absorption means behavioral contracts and verification loops are locally executable; it does not mean copying source-project facts or creating a second upstream truth source.

## Required Capability

- upstream design readback: use AcknowledgeBase as the design and domain-knowledge owner; absorb only de-identified, reusable contracts into the local Template Kernel.
- Research Contract: object, decision goal, depth, evidence plan, output shape, and no-op boundary.
- R2+ Source Plan checkpoint: required source classes, coverage target, contradiction plan, access boundary and stop condition are fixed before broad collection.
- Source Ledger and coverage matrix: external links, local raw / source ledger, read status, question coverage, contradictions, stale evidence and refresh trigger for durable research assets.
- currentness: verify release, license, platform, API, policy, security, and ecosystem facts before current claims.
- Judgment states: confirmed / observed / reported / inferred / blocked.
- Evidence Delta Re-open: supplemental material must be classified, externally checked, challenged with counter-evidence and propagated through the whole conclusion.
- So-What: state what a research result changes for template adoption, project design, or continued observation.
- deal-breaker: record license, supply-chain, privacy, cost, quality, or maintenance blockers.
- validation ladder: distinguish desk evidence, local validation, PoC, service/runtime readback and human approval; Adopt requires evidence appropriate to the claim scope.
- evaluation loop: deterministic contract validation, positive and negative fixtures, evaluator provenance, outcome review, revision brief and next-run decision.
- local research note: keep wiki-specific research outputs in local article, concept, project, or template owners.
- decision back-link: link adopted conclusions back to their owner page and upstream source.
- no-op: short-lived signals may remain out of durable docs if they do not change a decision.

## Upgrade Trigger

This repository already owns the reusable Template Kernel, so the local level is `strong-template-kernel`. It must not claim upstream design ownership, copy AcknowledgeBase domain content, or treat structural sensor success as research outcome proof.
